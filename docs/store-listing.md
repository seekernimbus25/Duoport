# Google Play store listing

Copy these into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

**App name** (30 max)
DualFrame: Multi-Format Camera

**Short description** (80 max)
Record once, get vertical, wide and square videos. Pro controls, LUTs, slow-mo.

**Full description** (4000 max)

Record one take and get every video shape you need: 9:16 for short vertical video,
16:9 for widescreen, 4:5 and 1:1 for feed posts, plus 3:4, 4:3 and 21:9. Each one is
saved as its own video at the resolution you choose, from 720p up to 4K.

FRAME EVERY SHAPE WHILE YOU SHOOT
• See every format as a coloured frame on the preview and drag each one into place
• Front + Back: record both cameras at once, with the second camera in the corner you choose
• Floating teleprompter with an eye-contact mode that keeps your eyes near the lens

AUTO, OR FULL MANUAL
• Auto: your phone's own camera look, with nothing to set up
• Pro: shutter (or shutter angle), ISO, Kelvin white balance and tint, manual focus,
  exposure, frame rate, and locking a specific lens
• Shutter priority and ISO priority, plus long-press AE/AF lock
• Zebras, focus peaking, false colour, histogram, horizon level, guides and audio meters

MADE FOR GRADING
• DF-Log profile, plus 10-bit HLG on phones that support it
• Built-in cinematic looks, and import your own .cube LUTs
• Export the DF-Log to Rec.709 LUT for your editing app

MOTION
• Slow motion at 60 fps, and 120 / 240 fps on phones that allow it
• Time-lapse and hyperlapse
• Stabilization: off, standard or enhanced

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.

The app checks what your phone's cameras can do and only shows the options that work
on it. Settings → This phone lists them.
