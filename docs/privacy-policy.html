DualFrame privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026

DualFrame is a camera app. It records video on your phone and turns each recording into the video shapes you choose. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: everything stays on your phone, and the developer receives nothing.

Information we collect

None. DualFrame has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no servers. It does not send any data over the internet.

Camera and microphone

The app asks for the camera and microphone only to record your videos. The picture and sound are processed on your phone and saved to your phone's storage (Movies/DualFrame). They are never uploaded by the app.

Your videos and files

  • Recordings and finished videos are saved in your phone's shared Movies folder, where you and your other apps (such as your gallery) can see, share or delete them.
  • Settings, teleprompter scripts and colour LUTs you import are stored privately inside the app on your phone. They are deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • When you tap Share, Android's share sheet sends the video to the app you choose. What that app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Sharing with third parties

DualFrame shares no information with anyone, because it collects none.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
